## About Me
I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of signal processing, artificial intelligence ethics, governance, explainability, and international standardization. My research focuses on trustworthy AI architectures, sociotechnical provenance, music information retrieval, and machine learning fairness.

---

## Research Interests
- **AI Ethics, Governance & Standardization**: Reciprocal AI frameworks, data sovereignty, risk-impact alignment with ISO standards, and trustworthy system architectures.
- **Audio & Signal Processing**: Music information retrieval (MIR), computational analysis of Carnatic music, time-frequency representations, and frequency-aware local explanations.
- **Explainability & Trustworthy AI (XAI)**: Interpretability methods (LIME/NMF tokenization), hallucination mitigation frameworks, and spectral forensics for synthetic media detection.
